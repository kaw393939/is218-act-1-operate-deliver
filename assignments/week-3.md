# Week 3: Author and review

[Act home](../README.md) · [Evidence template](../templates/evidence.md)

**Format:** Pair; individual evidence. **Preparation:** Lessons 5 and 6. **One weekly assignment.**

## Task

Use one pair-owned student repository for two rotated PR rounds. Preserve each prior individual repository. Shared integrated behavior earns the same 30-point evaluation for both; the remaining 70 points are evaluated per student from actual evidence. An absent partner requires an instructor-approved alternative, not a fabricated review.

## Submission

Submit through the instructor's announced LMS channel: repository URL, full final commit SHA, relevant evidence-file path, and PR/run links when required. Dates and visibility are set by the semester assignment announcement. Do not include credentials. Evidence must describe work actually performed.

## Example 100-point rubric

| Criterion | Points | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shared integrated behavior | 30 | Seven then eight passing cases; focused additions preserve prior behavior. |
| Individual authorship | 25 | Each student owns an issue, branch, PR, and response to feedback. |
| Individual review | 30 | Each student reviews the partner’s actual diff/checks and leaves actionable reasoning. |
| Integration evidence and disclosure | 15 | Post-merge SHA/result; individual assistance and contribution logs. |

Award full criterion credit for complete correct evidence, approximately half for partially correct/verified work, and zero for absent or unsupported evidence; explain partial credit against the listed criterion. Adjustments for course accommodations and deadlines follow published instructor policy. These assignment points do not define semester weighting.

## Allowed assistance and self-check

Follow [the assistance boundaries](../docs/assistance.md). Direct checkpoints are personal execution; bounded AI critique outside those blocks is disclosed and verified. Before submission ask: does the URL identify my work, does the SHA identify the final state, and can I explain one result without generated repair?

Peer checks should locate missing evidence early. An extra feature does not substitute for a required criterion. Record a block honestly and seek targeted help before the deadline.
